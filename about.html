PROJECT OVERVIEW

About S.A.L.S.A

Secure Acoustic Ledger For Subsea Autonomy — An intelligent, adaptive sonar transmitter payload for Autonomous Underwater Vehicles (AUVs).

🧠

Adaptive Intelligence

Continuous real-time sensing of turbidity, depth, and temperature selecting 100–500 kHz frequency in < 150 ms.

🌊

5 Waveform Modes

CW Pulse, LFM Up/Down Chirps, Barker-7 Phase Coded, and Geometric Exponential Sweeps.

🔋

56% Power Savings

Dynamic 35–80 mA draw and 80–240 MHz CPU scaling extending AUV mission life by up to 40%.

SYSTEM CAPABILITIES

Core Capabilities Dashboard

1. Environmental Sensing
Real-time turbidity, depth, and temperature capture via 12-bit ADC.
30-Sample Rolling Avg
2. Adaptive Decision Making
Autonomous frequency band selection across 5 discrete levels in < 150 ms.
100–500 kHz Range
3. Multi-Waveform Synthesis
DMA & hardware timers with Hamming window sidelobe suppression.
5 Modulations
4. 3-Stage Signal Conditioning
10µF DC blocker, 159 kHz RC low-pass, and LM358 op-amp buffer.
< 1% THD Output
5. Power Optimization
Dynamic CPU clock frequency scaling synchronized with waveform load.
80–240 MHz Clock
6. Live Telemetry OLED
SSD1306 128×64 I2C display showing frequency, power, and environment.
I2C 400 kHz
