Core Features & Waveforms

Visual breakdown of S.A.L.S.A's 7 core architectural capabilities and 5 distinct transmission waveform modes.

ARCHITECTURAL HIGHLIGHTS

7 Core Features Overview

⚡

1. Adaptive Switching

5 frequency bands (100, 200, 300, 400, 500 kHz) switched in < 150 ms based on water clarity.

100–500 kHz
🎯

2. 12-Bit DAC Output

External MCP4725 converter with 4096 voltage levels, 0–3.3V rail-to-rail, and 6µs settling time.

4096 Levels • I2C 400kHz
🌊

3. Multi-Waveform DSP

Synthesizes CW, LFM Up/Down, Barker-7, and Geometric Exponential Sweeps with Hamming windows.

5 DSP Modes
🔋

4. Power Management

Dynamic current (35–80 mA) and CPU scaling (80–240 MHz) yielding 56% energy savings.

+40% Mission Life
🎛️

5. 3-Stage Conditioning

10µF DC blocker, 159 kHz RC low-pass filter, and LM358 op-amp buffer for <1% THD.

SNR > 40 dB
📺

6. OLED Telemetry

SSD1306 128×64 display showing live environment, frequency, power %, current, and TX status.

Real-Time HUD
🔌

7. Modular Plug-and-Play Architecture

Custom 3D-printed enclosure, standard BNC connector, and compatibility with Pixhawk & ArduPilot drone flight controllers.

AUV Drop-In Payload
DSP MODULATION MODES

5 Waveform Transmission Modes

1. CW Pulse (Continuous Wave)

Narrowband

Fixed-frequency sine pulse for Doppler velocity measurement and rapid range profiling.

2. LFM Up Chirp (Linear Sweep)

High Resolution

Linear sweep from low to high frequency (100–500 kHz) for pulse-compressed shallow reef mapping.

3. LFM Down Chirp (Reverse Sweep)

Low Doppler Error

Reverse linear sweep eliminates range-Doppler coupling errors in dynamic subsea navigation.

4. Phase-Coded (Barker-7)

High Penetration

Binary phase modulation [+1, +1, +1, -1, -1, +1, -1] for extreme noise immunity in heavy mud.

5. Geometric Sweep (Exponential Chirp)

Constant Q

Exponential frequency chirp maintaining constant fractional bandwidth for wideband oceanographic surveys.
