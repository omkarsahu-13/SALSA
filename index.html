Traditional Sonar Payloads

Fixed Frequency (250kHz Compromise): Fails in murky water and loses resolution in clear reefs.
Continuous Full-Power Drain: Wastes battery capacity, cutting AUV mission runtime by 50%.
Requires Human Intervention: Cannot adapt autonomously to changing marine environments.

S.A.L.S.A Adaptive Solution

100–500 kHz Auto-Switching: High resolution in clear water + deep acoustic penetration in mud.
Dynamic Power Management: 35–80 mA current scaling provides 56% energy savings and +40% mission life.
