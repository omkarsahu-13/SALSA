PROPOSED ARCHITECTURE

The S.A.L.S.A Solution

A 4-step closed-loop adaptive pipeline: Sense, Decide, Generate, and Adapt executed in < 150 ms.

CLOSED-LOOP AUTONOMOUS FLOW

4-Step Intelligent Architecture

1

SENSE

Reads Turbidity (GPIO36), Depth (GPIO39), & Temp (25°C) with 12-bit ADC.

30-Sample Avg
2

DECIDE

ESP32 selects frequency (100–500kHz), power (35–80mA), and waveform mode.

Adaptive Logic
3

GENERATE

Digital synthesis + Hamming window ➔ 12-bit DAC ➔ 3-stage filter chain.

MCP4725 + LM358
4

ADAPT

Continuous closed-loop parameter recalibration in < 150 ms.

< 150 ms Loop
⚡

100–500 kHz Auto

First low-cost embedded system switching frequencies automatically in water.

🧠

Subsea Autonomy

Enables AUVs to independently modulate transmission without operator inputs.

📉

CPU Clock Scaling

ESP32 frequency (80–240 MHz) synced with DSP waveform generation load.

🔌

AUV Plug-and-Play

Compact modular design with standard BNC connector and 3D enclosure.
